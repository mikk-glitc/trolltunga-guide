Privacy Policy

Trolltunga Guide · Last updated: 29 October 2026

The app is built so that what you do with it stays on your phone.

Trolltunga Guide is published by Rafiki Digital (RAFIKI - DIGITAL - ELHOLM), a sole proprietorship registered in Norway, organisation number 938 727 856, Bekkjarvik. Rafiki Digital is responsible for this privacy policy.

1. What the app stores

The app stores your settings, your hike plan, your tracked hikes (time, distance, climb, steps and the GPS track), your passport stamps, photos and notes you add, and your game score.

All of this is stored only on your own phone. The app has no user accounts and no server of its own. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your location or personal data.

2. Location

With your permission the app uses your phone's GPS to show distances, your position on the map, the hike tracker, the camera navigation and to approve passport stamps. Your location is used on the phone only and is never sent to us. The app tracks hikes only while it is open and the tracker is running.

3. Camera, photos and step counter

  • Camera: to take a photo for a passport page, and for "Navigate with camera". Pictures are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Photos: only the picture you choose for a passport page.
  • Physical activity (step counter): only if you turn on the step counter in Settings.

4. Data that leaves your phone – only when you choose

  • Sharing: if you share a hike card, a diploma, a GPX file or your SOS position, your phone sends it with the app you choose.
  • Report a tip or fix: opens your own e-mail with a message you can read before you send it.

5. Weather

Forecasts come from MET Norway (api.met.no). The app asks for the weather at fixed places such as Trolltunga, Odda and Tyssedal, not at your own position.

6. Booking links

The app links to hotels, tours and other services, for example on Booking.com and GetYourGuide. Some are affiliate links marked "Ad": the partner can see that you came from our app and may pay us a small commission if you book. The price is the same for you. When you open a link you leave our app, and the partner's own privacy policy applies. We do not receive your name, contact details, payment details or what you book.

7. Purchases

If you unlock the full guide, the purchase is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

8. Analytics and advertising

The app does not use analytics tools, advertising networks or trackers, and does not show third-party ads.

9. Deleting your data

You can delete hikes, stamps and photos in the app. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone settings. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to delete on our side.

10. Children

The app is meant for adults and families planning a trip. It does not knowingly collect any data from children; all data stays on the device.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
